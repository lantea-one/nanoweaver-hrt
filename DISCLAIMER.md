## 2. Biosafety Level 1 (BSL-1) Materials & Non-Infectious Nature

1. **Non-Infectious Biomaterials**: All structural DNA origami models within this repository are based on single-stranded viral scaffold DNA (bacteriophage M13mp18) and synthetic chemical oligonucleotides (staples). The scaffold contains zero capsid proteins, zero viral envelope structures, and zero mammalian replication machinery. It is biologically incapable of infecting human or mammalian cells.
2. **BSL-1 Classification**: Wet-lab synthesis of DNA origami nanostructures operates strictly under **Biosafety Level 1 (BSL-1)** guidelines, utilizing non-hazardous, food-safe or molecular-grade nucleic acids, simple salt buffers (TAE, MgCl2), and non-toxic nucleic acid visualization stains (GelRed, SYBR Safe).
3. **Zero Biological Weapon or Dual-Use Classification**: This repository contains zero biological toxins, zero mammalian pathogens, zero gain-of-function viral modifications, and zero dual-use materials governed by the Biological Weapons Convention (BWC) or international export control regimes (e.g., Australia Group common control lists).

## 3. Compliance with Healthcare Privacy & Non-Discrimination Regulations

The cryptographic and data-handling subsystems in this codebase are engineered to uphold strict privacy standards:
* **HIPAA Safe Harbor (§ 164.514)**: Demonstrates algorithmic de-identification of health information.
* **GDPR Articles 9 & 17**: Demonstrates cryptographic consent verification and irreversible "Right to Erasure" key zeroization (Crypto-Shredding).
* **Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act (GINA)**: Restricts genomic marker processing strictly to scientific calibration.
* **FDA 21 CFR Part 11**: Implements append-only, tamper-evident cryptographic hash chains for research data provenance.

These compliance implementations are provided as architectural references and computational tools for researchers studying privacy-preserving bioinformatics.
